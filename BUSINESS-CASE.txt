RELAY AI — CUSTOMER INBOX TRIAGE: BUSINESS CASE
Week 2 Baseline Assessment — Business
Audience: non-technical executive (customer impact, business outcomes, strategy)
================================================================================


PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Relay AI sells one core promise: help small businesses handle more customer
messages without hiring more staff. That promise depends entirely on the triage
tool putting the RIGHT messages in front of agents FIRST.

Today, it does the opposite. The tool's prioritization was driven by surface
signals — punctuation and message length — instead of meaning. The result:

  - A real emergency like "Server down now" was marked LOW priority and sent to
    the bottom of the queue.
  - A friendly "Thank you so much!" was marked HIGH priority and jumped the line.
  - The same message could be sorted into different categories on different days,
    and a customer asking for a new feature was told to "check the billing portal."
  - When the AI was unavailable, the tool quietly showed a fabricated answer as
    if it were real.

For a product whose entire job is to prioritize and route, this is a direct hit
to the value customers pay for. The business risk is concrete: missed urgent
issues, breached response-time commitments, agents wasting time on the wrong
tickets, and erosion of trust in the AI — the exact opposite of the efficiency
Relay AI is selling.


SOLUTION STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I rebuilt the parts of the tool that decide WHAT MATTERS and WHAT TO DO ABOUT IT:

  1. Prioritization now reads meaning, not punctuation. Genuine urgency signals
     (outage, can't access, breach, payment failed) push a message up; polite,
     low-stakes language pushes it down. Emergencies surface, thank-you notes
     don't.

  2. Categories are now consistent and predictable. The AI is constrained to a
     fixed, business-defined set of categories and returns the same answer for
     the same message every time — no more drift.

  3. Recommended actions are correct and priority-aware. Each category maps to
     the right next step, and high-urgency messages are automatically flagged for
     escalation so they bypass the standard queue.

  4. The tool is now honest when the AI is unavailable. Instead of silently
     faking a result, it clearly flags "AI unavailable" so agents know to trust
     their own judgment.

These changes are tested and live on a review branch, with automated checks
confirming the behavior.


WHY THIS DELIVERS THE MOST VALUE TO RELAY AI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - It protects the core value proposition. Correct prioritization IS the
    product. Fixing the inverted urgency turns the tool from a liability that
    misroutes tickets into the asset customers are paying for — the single
    highest-leverage fix available.

  - It directly reduces customer churn risk. Faster response to true emergencies
    means fewer breached SLAs and fewer frustrated end-customers for the small
    businesses Relay serves — the outcome that keeps them subscribed.

  - It advances the "more volume, no new headcount" strategy. Accurate first-pass
    triage means agents spend their time on the tickets that actually need them,
    increasing throughput per agent without added cost.

  - It rebuilds trust in the AI. Consistent categories plus honest fallback
    behavior make the tool feel reliable. For a paid SaaS, unpredictable or
    fabricated output is a silent trust-killer that quietly drives cancellations.

  - It's high-impact and low-cost. These are small, focused, well-tested changes
    — fast to ship and easy to tune per customer later — delivering outsized
    business return relative to the engineering effort.
